# Bangladesh SME Finance Copilot

Agreed product specification and pilot operating plan. Research recorded **17 October 2026**; implementation notes updated **19 October 2026**. Read together with [DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md](DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md). This plan applies to every contributor and agent.

## Promise and first audience

**Understand how your business is doing, decide what to do next, and get help completing it.**

Start with small shops and traders in **Dhaka South**, including informal businesses, women owners, people with different literacy levels, and owners with incomplete records. An owner should be able to start without uploading registration papers. The product is for someone doing the work alone, without an accountant on staff.

This is a real product pilot, not a loan marketing funnel. Useful completed actions, understanding and fewer missed commitments are success measures. Loan uptake, referral revenue and message volume are not. Do not rank financing by referral payments.

Simple bookkeeping, dues, backups and reminders are foundations already offered by local products. Our additional value is connecting records to a decision and helping complete the next useful action. The cited informal-enterprise survey describes three survey areas, not a nationwide estimate. Research does not establish which wording owners understand or what they will pay; observation must test those questions.

## Owner problems and outcomes

| Owner question | Required outcome |
|---|---|
| How is the business doing? | One short explanation, uncertainty when records are missing, and one next step. |
| Where did the money go? | Separate stock, expenses, repayments, transfers and household withdrawals; show supporting records on demand. |
| Who owes me? | Track credit sales, partial payments, promises and disputes; prepare a reviewed collection message and follow-up. |
| Can I pay what is coming up? | Dated expected receipts and payments, the likely gap, and practical responses. |
| I need money | Establish purpose, amount, deadline, essential commitments and debt before suggesting two or three routes. |
| Should I take this loan? | Compare an actual complete quote with the owner's cash commitments and reserve, separately from eligibility. |
| Should I buy a new product? | A budgeted small experiment, an alternative purchase, or a reason to wait. |
| What paperwork is missing? | An applicable checklist, evidence for deadlines, a reviewed handoff and tracked completion. |
| Can you prepare this? | An editable draft, destination, required official form where applicable, and next step. |

## Product experience

Preserve six destinations (owner decision, 19 October 2026):

| Destination | Contents |
|---|---|
| Home | Exactly two cards: recorded money and one next step, with one primary action. Only the agreed closed Prices edge drawer and occasional 10-second note are exceptions; see the design principles. |
| AI Assistant | Chat for understanding the problem, with resumable guided tasks beside it. Picking a task preserves the conversation. |
| Tasks | To do, Reminders, Coming up; saved guided cases and follow-ups. |
| Money | This month and Money ahead; decision tools linked from the relevant context. |
| Records | Add, review, correct, import and reconcile records. |
| More | People, documents, stock, business details, support and specialist tools. |

The AI Assistant combines chat with a guided task panel (owner decision, 19 October 2026). Financial actions still follow the guided pattern:

**Understand the goal → confirm known facts → ask the next useful question → compare options → prepare an action → review the outcome.**

One question at a time; at most three choices shown. Use buttons, amounts, dates, selected records and document checklists. Offer “I don't know” and “Do this later.” Unknown is not zero. Reuse records as suggestions, and let the owner check or replace stale facts. After six new answers, show a summary and offer continuing or pausing. Backtracking, edits and resuming must retain progress. Explanations and calculations belong behind disclosure controls.

The interface starts in plain English. More languages on Login and in Settings enables বাংলা and 中文; switching it off returns to English. Preserve original names and in-progress authentication input, without storing passwords. Optional speech fills editable input and never sends a message or executes a command automatically. Names, dates and amounts need review. Do not promise speech without its required connectivity.

## Financial requirements

### Records and reconciliation

Distinguish cash sales and credit sales; purchases and the cost of stock actually sold; borrowing and sales; principal and financing charges; business expenses and household withdrawals; transfers and income/spending; advances and completed sales; returns, refunds, damage and partial payments. Use questions such as “Was money paid now?” instead of accounting labels.

Allow daily totals. Product profit requires quantities and costs. A cash total is neither an accrual profit statement nor a live bank balance. Receipt/khata extraction and voice must be reviewed before saving. Reconciliation compares counted money to records; a mismatch creates a correction task, not an invented balancing transaction or a confident health judgement.

### Funding routes

First establish purpose, amount, date, available money after essentials, existing debt and realistic customer collections. Consider a smaller batch, delay, supplier terms and customer preorders before borrowing. Preorders require a delivery/refund plan and enough money to fulfil them.

Cover commercial-bank SME desks, women-entrepreneur products, Islamic financing using the actual contract, SME Foundation partner institutions, PKSF partner routes, MRA-licensed MFIs and an existing City Bank/bKash offer only when the owner is eligible. Do not assume a programme is open or that the advertised maximum is an offer. Verify grants individually. Partner programmes do not imply a direct application to the sponsoring foundation or guaranteed funding.

For every candidate establish actual terms, net proceeds, all charges, each repayment date, security/guarantor consequences, documents, processing time and local availability. An enquiry route may be shown before verification, but must not provide a confident rate, deadline or recommendation.

### Loan checks

Use cash actually received, seasonality, essential business spending, necessary household withdrawals, formal and informal debts, taxes, rent, supplier commitments, purchase costs, net proceeds and a reserve chosen by the owner. Include weekly instalments, grace periods and final balloon payments across the **whole term**. Compare total taka repaid, net proceeds, dates and fees before technical rate terminology.

Outcomes: **Looks manageable under these assumptions**, **Repayments look too tight**, or **We need more information**. Show one reason, relevant amounts and an action. Missing debt, essentials, opening money or quote terms prevents an affirmative result. Eligibility and affordability are separate. Never infer local demand or lender approval.

The implemented cash scenario reserves each 30-day period's recurring business, household and existing-debt costs upfront. It uses steady daily receipts or the owner's dated seasonal receipts. The slower case uses the owner's lower monthly receipt assumption and a seven-day receipt delay. Additional dated commitments and the proposed quote are charged on their actual dates. This deliberately conservative reserve method is a scenario, not a prediction or a substitute for practitioner review. Do not count a debt both in the monthly reserve and additional dated commitments. Changing assumptions or records invalidates previews and marks saved comparisons stale. Full loan calendars can be entered manually or generated from equal weekly/monthly instalments and then edited.

### New-product experiments

Ask about purchase, delivery/setup and selling costs, price, quantity, demand evidence, collection timing, unsold stock, spoilage, returns and storage. Show upfront money, money left, contribution per item, break-even units for additional fixed costs and slower sales. The implemented downside sells half the expected quantity and treats unsold stock as producing no cash. Expected demand comes from the owner or identified records, never the model.

Offer a smaller test, supplier discussion or waiting. Preorders are an alternative to investigate, not a guaranteed funding source. Save test quantity, budget and review date; later capture actual units and money received. A smaller batch is still blocked when it would breach the chosen reserve or has non-positive per-item contribution. The stock register remains the place for detailed inventory and adjustments.

## Government rules and paperwork

Determine applicability from location, actual activity, registration, business structure and period. Distinguish registration, filing and payment. Do not apply a universal trade-licence renewal date, fee or document checklist. Start with a reviewed DSCC process. Use current NBR material for income tax and VAT/turnover tax; do not hardcode stale thresholds or extensions.

Employees, premises, imports/manufacturing, regulated products and online selling introduce separate applicability checks. DIFE, BSTI, BFSA, Fire Service and DBID are relevant authorities only when the facts make them relevant. Nationwide, manufacturing, importing, full payroll and direct financial integrations are outside this first pilot.

A complete handoff explains why the task applies, what is available/missing, the prescribed form, an editable supporting letter, the verified portal/office and responsible role, submission follow-up and receipt storage. Phone preparation may lead to a desktop/office step; the NBR e-Return FAQ identifies mobile limitations. Generated documents are **Prepared**, owner-reported submission with a reference is **Submitted**, and acknowledgement of acceptance is **Accepted/completed**. A PDF is not submission. Payment and filing occur through authorised external channels.

The current pack intentionally produces a **request for guidance**, evidence checklist and source links while human verification is pending. It does not claim to reproduce a prescribed government application, determine personalised tax liability, or submit a filing. During the pilot, a qualified reviewer supplies applicable requirements and checks each personalised conclusion before reliance.

## Verified information register

Machine-readable enquiry routes are in [config/pilot_sources.yaml](../config/pilot_sources.yaml). Each published entry needs exact URL, responsible authority/provider, activity and location applicability, required documents, submission destination, effective dates, review date, reviewer and reviewed content digest. Dates and amounts quoted by owners must identify their quote/document separately from an official rule.

All initial entries are **needs review**. Recording a URL or fetching a page is not legal/financial verification. Published status requires a reviewer, effective dates, weekly review freshness, a matching reviewed digest and a recent automated check. Changed or unavailable content is flagged. Daily checks fetch only configured URLs, bound download size, and do not silently follow redirects or publish changes. A redirected page must be inspected and its canonical URL updated by the reviewer. Dynamic page changes can create false alarms; human review resolves these. A paper/loan preview fingerprints current source metadata as well as business records.

## Software implementation and boundaries

The existing FastAPI app, SQLAlchemy database, role permissions, records, audit log and independently running shared-model service remain in use. Additive pilot tables are created by `init_db`; back up the database and encryption key before a real deployment.

| Capability | Current implementation |
|---|---|
| Six workflows | Business-scoped create/resume, answer, pause/checkpoint, preview and owner-confirmation APIs; single-question UI. |
| Financial calculation | Deterministic money validation, full quote schedules, slow-sales/delayed-receipt scenarios, shortfall and product calculations. Null/NaN/invalid dates rejected or handled as missing. |
| Record actions | Cash/credit/partial sales and purchases; loans, split principal/charges, withdrawals, paired transfers, advances/refunds; reconciliation correction reminders. Product-cost gaps flagged. |
| Persistence and stale state | Optimistic versions; answer edits invalidate previews; record/source fingerprints; transactional effects; repeated confirmed requests do not duplicate records/documents/reminders. |
| Guided model assistance | Optional bounded shared-local-model selection among approved applicable question IDs and goal categories, with rules fallback. Chat uses the chosen provider through the permission and confirmation services. Model has no independent financial execution authority. Free text is not sent to the question-routing helper. |
| Documents | Encrypted PDF/image attachments and editable text drafts, downloadable/printable to PDF, scoped access, receipt references, distinct handoff states. |
| Reminders | Persistent in-app alerts and jobs, snooze/date/complete controls, independent worker, owner preferences and delivery records. |
| SMS | SSL adapter implemented; off by default. Requires configuration, consent and operator phone verification. No customer SMS sending. |
| Verified sources | Dated directory, review metadata, daily content-change checker and stale/unavailable states. Initial content remains enquiry-only. |
| Offline browser | Installable shell, owner-scoped IndexedDB drafts and cached guided progress; explicit manual sync for server review. No cached API responses, automatic confirmation or offline SMS/filing. |
| Privacy/help | Existing owner export/deletion now includes guided cases, attachments, jobs and preferences. Support contact configured by the pilot operator. |

Legacy financing/rule tools use illustrative content. They are blocked by default (`ALLOW_ILLUSTRATIVE_GUIDANCE=false`), and generic compliance dates no longer generate alerts. The flag exists for isolated demonstrations/tests only and must never be enabled for a real-owner pilot.

The model is optional: records, guided questions and money calculations work with it unavailable. Offline case copies are dated snapshots, not refreshed records. A pending offline answer uses the version it was based on; conflicts require review rather than overwriting newer work. Branching to a new set of questions requires synchronisation. Local drafts belong to the signed-in owner; session expiry locks them until sign-in, while explicit sign-out removes that owner's local copies. Browser storage is not a server backup and may be cleared by the device. Offline reopening of the active signed-in browser session is supported; a new session still requires online authentication. Use HTTPS for installation outside localhost.

Detailed inventory/returns/damage management, photo extraction, import deduplication, invoice payments and corrections continue through the existing Records/Stock tools. The guided totals flow does not fabricate item quantities or COGS. Do not use an incomplete stock ledger for a confident product-profit claim. Cloud OCR/speech availability depends on the configured provider/browser.

## Running and operating the pilot

From the repository root, with the environment installed:

```powershell
# Website; does not need to start another model for each user
.\scripts\start_website.ps1

# Independent reminder/source-check worker (same database and encryption key)
.\scripts\start_reminder_worker.ps1

# Optional single cycle or operating report
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m sme_copilot.pilot.worker --once
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m sme_copilot.pilot.worker --report
```

Run the worker continuously under the deployment's service supervisor, with restart-on-failure. On Windows this can be a scheduled task running the worker script at system startup, without a visible window and with automatic restart. On Linux use the existing deployment's process manager. Both processes must use the same `DATABASE_URL`, `FIELD_ENCRYPTION_KEY` and configuration. Do not start duplicate web workers against test data or use the demo seed/reset command on owner data. No scheduler or server is installed as a background operating-system service by this change.

Configuration:

```dotenv
ALLOW_ILLUSTRATIVE_GUIDANCE=false
PILOT_SOURCE_CHECKS=true
PILOT_QUESTION_MODEL=true
PILOT_HELP_CONTACT=Your real pilot facilitator contact
PILOT_SMS_ENABLED=false
PILOT_SMS_API_TOKEN=
PILOT_SMS_SID=
```

The provider catalog defaults to Claude; `.env.example` and existing shared-service installations explicitly choose `LLM_PROFILE=shared-llm`. That shared Gemma profile remains supported independently of the website. Use `rules-offline` for deterministic operation. The optional guided-question helper uses only local profiles and approved field IDs. Setup and market-item selection can use the chosen cloud profile; their text prompts follow `MASK_PII`, with a request-local vault that restores responses only on this computer. A local model never falls back to a cloud one.

Before enabling SMS: contract with the provider, register sender ID, whitelist the server IP, verify credentials, test delivery/failure semantics with an authorised test number, establish the controlled allowance and add monitoring. The owner saves their number and opt-in in Tasks → Reminders → SMS and reminder settings. Verify number ownership out of band, then record the operator and evidence:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m sme_copilot.pilot.worker --verify-owner 1 --reviewer "Pilot operator" --note "Ownership confirmed with the owner during onboarding"
```

The worker uses Bangladesh time and the owner's selected hour. Suggested offsets are 30/7/1 days for evidenced renewal deadlines and 3/1 days for payments, plus the due date. It sends at most one generic grouped owner SMS per day, with no customer names or money amounts. Consent or phone changes are rechecked before sending. Paid invoices and completed/dismissed tasks stop reminders. The owner can snooze, change the date or unsubscribe.

Jobs and deliveries survive process restarts. A database claim and daily unique key prevent concurrent duplicates. Connection failures known to precede a request retry up to three times; timeouts or ambiguous sends become **unknown** and need provider reconciliation. A crashed in-flight attempt is not blindly resent. Provider acceptance, handset delivery and owner completion remain separate. A late worker restart does not silently send outside the selected hour; monitor missed opportunities in the operating report. Accepted/unknown delivery outcomes can be reconciled from a provider delivery report:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m sme_copilot.pilot.worker --delivery 1 --status delivered --reviewer "Pilot operator" --note "Provider delivery report reference checked"
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m sme_copilot.pilot.worker --review-case 1 --reviewer "Qualified reviewer" --note "Quote/requirements, completeness and relevant dates checked; record the conclusion here"
```

These commands create audit entries; they do not contact owners or simulate human verification. Run `--report` daily to inspect pending reviews, changed/unavailable sources and unconfirmed/failed delivery states. Weekly, an accountable local reviewer inspects the relevant official notices, records the exact applicability/effective dates in the source register and updates the reviewed digest. Reverify an actual lending offer before a borrowing decision. Do not publish an entry merely to clear an alert.

Export is owner-only in Settings. Create a consistent SQLite backup with `.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\backup_pilot.py PATH_TO_NEW_BACKUP.db`; the script checks integrity and refuses to overwrite an existing file. Keep the matching field-encryption key separately in a protected secret store. Test recovery using a copy on an isolated instance with the same key, verifying records and opening an encrypted document. For a real restore, stop the website and worker, retain the existing database and its WAL/SHM files as a rollback set, and point `DATABASE_URL` to the verified restored copy before restarting. Never combine a restored database with old WAL/SHM files. A JSON export includes documents and cases but is not an executable restore script. Deletion deactivates business accounts and removes pilot data; the existing minimal audit history is retained. There is no automatic external sharing. A working support contact and tested recovery are pilot launch requirements.

## Pilot sequence and acceptance

**Stage 1: observe 12 owners.** Observe real sales entry, collections, stock purchases and paperwork in Dhaka South. Include women owners, different literacy levels and incomplete records. Obtain consent for observation; record errors, time and explanation in the owner's own words. An accounting practitioner reviews decision math; a qualified local reviewer checks the initial compliance pack. These are product operating responsibilities, not a requirement that every owner hire an accountant.

**Stage 2: validate the smallest complete experience.** Exercise all six workflows with resuming, documents, in-app reminders, consented owner SMS and actual phone/browser connectivity loss. The software provides those flows and gated adapters; verified compliance packs, live provider onboarding and human review remain launch prerequisites. Keep broader sectors and integrations out of the first release.

**Stage 3: six weeks with 20 owners.** Free pilot with a controlled SMS allowance. Review every borrowing comparison and personalised compliance conclusion. Capture support time and real costs before selecting pricing. Interviews, recruitment, human reviews, live SMS validation and the six-week study have **not** been performed by implementing the code.

Proposed targets:

- At least 80% complete a basic task unaided after onboarding.
- Typical record entry under 30 seconds; measure it rather than assume it.
- At least 80% explain the next step in their own words.
- Owners distinguish money received, money owed and expected future money.
- Zero duplicate confirmed actions and zero unauthorised external sharing.
- Every loan comparison traces to an actual quote or currently reviewed terms.
- Every regulatory deadline traces to applicable official evidence or an owner-provided document.
- The operating team can see missing information, source changes and delivery failures.

Use a consented pilot worksheet with participant pseudonym, workflow, start/end time, facilitator interventions, owner explanation, understanding of cash/dues/future receipts, completion/follow-up, overwhelm rating, support minutes and incurred cost. Review weekly retention, missed commitments and useful completed actions. Do not collect unnecessary personal data in the study notes.

Failure scenarios to rehearse: credit sale mistaken for cash; duplicate statement import; partial payment; household withdrawal; weekly debt and balloon repayment; seasonal receipts; missing product costs; uncertain loan fees; changed deadlines; repeated confirmation; failed/ambiguous SMS; interrupted workflows; language switching; offline reload; stale sync; account switching; data export/recovery/deletion. Automated coverage lives in `tests/test_pilot.py` alongside the existing finance, import, security and UI tests. Rendered checks must cover English/Bangla, narrow screens, light/dark, keyboard and reduced motion.

Release readiness is demonstrated by useful actions completed with little help, not by the number of screens implemented.
